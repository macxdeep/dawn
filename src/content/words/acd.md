a melody running in my head
battle of angels and demons lead 
it was always there.

a machine of greens, blues, and red 
screening you, your sins, and bless 
keeping everything in balance.

cries of A and D everywhere
not letting me sleep well 
dilemma raised to the x.

to set this war down, all you need is now
put your attention to the tune first
by defying Angels Choiring with Demons.
