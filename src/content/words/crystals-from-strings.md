title: Chiaroscuro
date: 2023-04-03T00:00:00
---

morning of sunday
found a sound
clinking in the attic,
time has come around 
for dusting the steels
rusting in the attic.
surprise to the ear
on the pluck of a string,
heard crystals falling.
chimes took a ride
around the past time
mesmerized me, in the attic.
