---
title: DIM THIS?
date: 2025-03-18T00:00:00
---

this is how it goes
found me in front of a door
heard it banging with knocks
apparently it sounded like a rhythm of a sort

rather than questioning who this is for
let the ear-candy bliss the whole
let it play, let it unfold
why dim this after all?

neither submit nor resist
let it invite you to play a tune of your own
neither collapse nor think of rebuilding
just play along, a tune of your own
