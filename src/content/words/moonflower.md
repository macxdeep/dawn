title: Moonflower
date: 2026-03-27T12:00:00
---

land of white
disguise of night
everything seems quiet
oh yes, the paradise
eyes following the blind
curtains over the mind
screams dragging him down
deeper in the well of fright
to seek, to cry or to fly
gray matter tensing tight
humming the hymns for light
revealed by the presence of the moon
awry in the heart and mind
but who is hindering the truth? 
shush it me, myself and I
