hey i've got to tell something off my chest
you don't have to listen to what they say
can't preach things that i haven't learned
yet but still i will be around the corner, just give me that gaze

time is a thing, it might scare you off
live present before the bubble gets burst
someone in the street please shout my name
i want that every little small talk

heard and felt that the world is kind
but you have to participate too
to get that taste of pudding pie
be nice enough to bring your own spoon

keep an eye on your heart and mind
before they begin to blame and fight
harness harmony by loving life
keeping no shades and some secrets alive
