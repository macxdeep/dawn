title: Chiaroscuro
date: 2026-09-05T12:00:00
---

with closed eyes in blank abyss
wizard is posed

bright and dark stars are choiring
bliss and mourn

a visual hits his mood
from across

a self-shining mountain?
or a hole in the soul?
stone abiding in dark?
or being blessed with light?

what is the truth?
